# Sound Files Setup

## Required Sound Files

Place these MP3 files in `static/sounds/`:

1. **click.mp3** - Button click sound
2. **success.mp3** - Correct guess sound
3. **wrong.mp3** - Wrong guess sound
4. **thinking.mp3** - Background thinking sound

## Free Sound Resources

Download free sounds from:
- https://freesound.org/
- https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/

## Quick Setup (Use Web Sounds)

If you don't have sound files, the app will work silently.
Sounds are optional and enhance the experience.

## Recommended Sounds

- **click.mp3**: Short "pop" or "click" (0.1s)
- **success.mp3**: Victory fanfare or "ding" (1-2s)
- **wrong.mp3**: Buzzer or "oops" sound (0.5s)
- **thinking.mp3**: Soft ambient music (optional)
