## 🎯 High Priority Features (Easy to Add)

### 1. **Streak Counter & Statistics** ⭐⭐⭐
Track user performance across games:
- Games played
- Win streak
- Total correct guesses
- Average questions per game
- Best time

**Why:** Gamification keeps users engaged

### 2. **Share Results** 📱⭐⭐⭐
Share game results on social media:
- "I guessed Cristiano Ronaldo in 8 questions! 🎯"
- Generate shareable image/text
- Twitter, WhatsApp, Facebook integration

**Why:** Viral marketing, user engagement

### 3. **Daily Challenge** 🏆⭐⭐
One specific player per day:
- Everyone gets the same player
- Global leaderboard for that day
- Rewards for fastest/fewest questions

**Why:** Brings users back daily

### 4. **Multiplayer Mode** 👥⭐⭐
Two players compete:
- Who can guess their player faster
- Real-time or turn-based
- Local or online

**Why:** Social engagement, competitive fun

### 5. **Player Images** 🖼️⭐⭐⭐
Show player photo when guessed correctly:
- More satisfying win experience
- Visual confirmation
- Better UX

**Why:** Visual feedback is more engaging

### 6. **Sound Effects** 🔊⭐
Add subtle sounds:
- Click sounds for buttons
- Success sound when correct
- Thinking sound for genie
- Background music (optional)

**Why:** More immersive experience

### 7. **Difficulty Levels** 🎚️⭐⭐
- Easy: Only famous players (Messi, Ronaldo, etc.)
- Medium: Current players
- Hard: Includes retired/deceased players
- Expert: All players including obscure ones

**Why:** Caters to different skill levels

### 8. **Categories/Themes** 🎭⭐⭐
Specialized game modes:
- Premier League only
- World Cup winners
- Ballon d'Or winners
- Current season transfers
- Legends only
- Young talents

**Why:** Variety keeps it interesting

- "Speed Demon" - Guess in under 5 questions
- "Know-It-All" - Guess 10 players correctly
- "Legend Hunter" - Guess 5 retired players
- "Transfer Expert" - Guess recent transfers

**Why:** Progression system, replay value

### 10. **Time Attack Mode** ⏱️
Race against the clock:
- 60 seconds to guess
- Bonus points for speed
- Leaderboard

**Why:** Adds urgency and excitement

### 11. **Guess History** 📊
Show past games:
- Players you've guessed
- Questions asked
- Success rate per league/position

**Why:** Personal analytics, learning

### 12. **Custom Player Lists** ✏️
Let users create custom lists:
- "My favorite players"
- "Premier League 2024"
- Share lists with friends

**Why:** Personalization, community content

### 13. **Progressive Web App (PWA)** 📲

- Works offline
- App icon on home screen
- Push notifications

**Why:** Better mobile experience
