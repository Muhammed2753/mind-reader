# 🎮 Football Guessing Game - Top Notch Features

## 🔥 MUST-HAVE Features

### 1. **Smart AI Question System**
- AI suggests best questions based on remaining players
- Question efficiency scoring (eliminates most players)
- Adaptive difficulty levels

### 2. **Multiplayer Mode**
- Real-time 1v1 battles
- Leaderboards & rankings
- Friend challenges

### 3. **Gamification**
- Daily challenges & rewards
- Achievement system (badges)
- Streak tracking
- XP & level progression
- Coins/currency system

### 4. **Enhanced UI/UX**
- Player card animations
- Smooth transitions
- Sound effects & music
- Haptic feedback
- Dark/Light themes
- Player reveal animations

### 5. **Game Modes**
- Classic Mode (current)
- Time Attack (60 seconds)
- Unlimited Questions
- Guess in 5 Questions
- Team Mode (guess team first)
- League Mode (only one league)
- Mystery Mode (blurred player image)

### 6. **Social Features**
- Share results to social media
- Screenshot game results
- Compare stats with friends
- Global chat

### 7. **Data Enhancements**
- Real player photos (API integration)
- Player stats (goals, assists)
- Recent form & news
- Transfer history
- Career highlights

### 8. **Smart Features**
- Hint system (costs coins)
- 50/50 elimination
- Skip player option
- Question history
- Smart filtering

### 9. **Analytics & Stats**
- Personal statistics dashboard
- Win rate tracking
- Average questions used
- Favorite leagues/teams
- Time played

### 10. **Monetization (Optional)**
- Premium leagues (La Liga, Premier League)
- Ad-free experience
- Exclusive player packs
- Custom themes
